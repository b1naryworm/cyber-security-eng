Comprehensive systems where hardware, software, and your habits work as a single mechanism.

PACKAGE 01
Infrastructure Integrity

Stationary protection of living and working spaces

In simple terms: We turn your home or office into a digital bunker. The provider does not know what you are doing online, a hacker cannot "jump" from the TV to your computer, and all your passwords and documents are stored physically in your room, not on Google or Apple servers.

Network Perimeter (VLAN Segmentation)

Deep network segmentation. We isolate "dirty" devices (Smart TV, cameras, robot vacuums) from your workstations. Even if your camera is hacked, the attacker cannot access data on the same network.

Router-Level Double-VPN

All home internet traffic is encrypted at the router level and routed through a chain of two independent servers in different countries. This hides the very fact of using protection from the ISP (DPI) and ensures 100% anonymity for all devices (even those where VPN cannot be installed).

Digital Sovereignty (Self-Hosted Cloud)

Deployment of a personal password server (Vaultwarden) and cloud (Nextcloud). You get full functionality of iCloud/Google Drive, but with zero risk of data leakage to corporations or intelligence agencies.

DNS Sinkhole & AdBlock

System on-the-fly analyzes all outgoing device requests and blocks ads, trackers, and telemetry before they even load. Devices stop sending activity reports to the manufacturer.

Package technology stack:

MikroTik RB5009 (Enterprise Router) Raspberry Pi 5 (Server) UniFi 6 Lite (Secure AP) Docker & WireGuard-Kernel

2. Ultimate laptop hardening (Workstation Hardening)

We turn an ordinary laptop into a digital fortress, protected from physical intrusion, data theft, and hidden espionage.

  • OS and isolation: Qubes OS or Hardened Debian with AppArmor/SELinux.
  • Firmware: Coreboot/Libreboot + Measured Boot (TPM 2.0) + own Secure Boot keys.

3. Acoustic protection (Anti-eavesdropping)

Microphone blockers (Mic Lock): Using physical plugs for the 3.5mm port or software kill switches. The system "thinks" an external headset without a microphone is connected — this hardware-blocks the ability to record sound through built-in microphones of a smartphone or laptop.

Ultrasonic tracking protection: Blocking frequencies used by marketing beacons that transmit hidden signals between your TV and smartphone.

4. Automated metadata scrubbing (Deep Metadata Scrubbing)

For Mobile: Setting up automatic removal of all data (GPS, date, device model) each time a photo is saved or sent.

For Desktop: Setting up a "Hot Folder" via the MAT2 utility. Any document (PDF, DOCX) or image placed in this folder instantly becomes anonymous — all hidden metadata is automatically destroyed.

5. Identity masking (Email & Identity Masking)

Implementation of SimpleLogin / AnonAddy system. You no longer expose your real email anywhere. A unique alias is created for each site, forwarding emails to you. This breaks the link between all your accounts — even if one account is compromised, the rest remain untouched.

6. Personal Double-VPN with obfuscation

Essence: A private chain of two independent servers in different countries (e.g., Switzerland → Iceland).

How it works: The first server knows your IP, the second knows your traffic. Neither has complete information about you. This is 10 times more reliable than public VPNs.

Obfuscation: Masking traffic as regular HTTPS (the provider sees "YouTube browsing", not VPN).

Kill Switch: Instant internet blocking if the connection drops.

Technologies: Rental of personal VPS, WireGuard / VLESS / Shadowsocks protocols. Result: complete invisibility to the provider and protection against de-anonymization.
